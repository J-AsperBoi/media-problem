The bottleneck is us.

The answer takes days. Getting it to everyone takes years. Humans aren't slow; our coordination is. The fixes to most of our problems already exist somewhere, stuck between the people who have them and the people who need them.

We're working on the speed of human coordination: faster ways for people, and the tools they choose to work with, to find each other, decide together, and act while it still matters.
