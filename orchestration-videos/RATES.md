## AI (fast clocks)

- **Autonomous task length (METR "time horizon")**: the length of task, measured in human expert time, that frontier AI agents complete with 50% success. Doubled roughly every 7 months from 2019 to 2024; METR's January 2026 update (Time Horizon 1.1) estimates a post-2023 doubling time of 130.8 days (about 4.3 months). Source: METR, metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1; summarized on Wikipedia "METR".
- **Where it stands, and what changed in the last three months**: August 2025 frontier ≈ 3.5 hours (TH1.1). Early 2026 ≈ 12 hours. As of Aug 29, 2026, the top published value is about 17.4 hours, added May 2026, and the series has been effectively pinned at the benchmark's ceiling since; METR warns estimates above 16 hours are unreliable because the task suite is saturating, and is shifting toward new metrics. In plain words: over summer 2026 the measuring stick ran out before the capability did. Sources: ai2027-tracker.com (May 2026), FutureSearch forecast note citing metr.org (Aug 2026).
- **Cost of AI at fixed capability**: falling about 40x per year, i.e. halving roughly every 2 months, though unevenly (90% range roughly 10x to 900x per year depending on task). Source: Epoch AI "Trends in AI" dashboard, updated Feb 2026; Epoch AI "LLM inference price trends".
- **AI chip compute stock**: growing about 3.4x per year (doubling every ~6.8 months). **Frontier training compute**: about 5x per year. Source: Epoch AI dashboard, Feb 2026.
- **Science example**: after the SARS-CoV-2 sequence was shared on Jan 11, 2020, NIH and Moderna finalized the vaccine sequence on Jan 13, 2020 (2 days); first clinical batch Feb 7; first trial dose Mar 16, 2020. Source: Moderna SEC filing, 2020.

## Human institutions (slow clocks, high variance)

- **US federal environmental impact statements**: mean 4.5 years from notice of intent to record of decision (2010–2017); half took over 3 years 7 months; a quarter took over 6 years; 36 finished within a year while dozens took a decade or more. For EISs finalized in 2024, the median from notice to final EIS was 2.2 years. Sources: CEQ EIS Timelines Fact Sheet (Dec 2018); CEQ EIS Timeline Report (Jan 13, 2025); Center for Growth and Opportunity summary of CEQ data.
- **Updating the US Constitution**: 27 amendments; the most recent, the 27th, was proposed in 1789 and ratified in 1992, 202 years later. No amendment since 1992. (Well-established historical record; cite the National Archives if shown on screen.)
- **Vaccine distribution inequity**: May 2021, low-income countries had received 0.2% of global COVID vaccine doses while holding about 10% of the world's population; 9 new "vaccine billionaires" held $19.3B combined, enough to fully vaccinate low-income countries 1.3 times. Source: People's Vaccine Alliance analysis of Forbes data, May 20, 2021 (label as an advocacy group's analysis).
